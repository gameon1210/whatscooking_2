Suggested repair order

  1. Reliability and access: setup drafts, true history editing, switch state/keyboard support and close targets.
  2. First-use experience: result-led welcome, explicit dietary setup, visible skip/progress, simpler Today hierarchy and clear action names.
  3. Delight and completion: complete theme system, genuine family joining, country-aware contacts, deeper cuisines and trustworthy reports.
Limits: expert browser review with synthetic local data. No physical-device testing, actual messages, paid AI requests or production source changes.
