## Added after review: your own dishes in setup

- The favourites step no longer dead-ends when a dish isn't listed. Searching shows “Did you mean” (typo-tolerant, e.g. “popha” → Poha) and **Add “…” as our dish**, or “Don’t see a dish you make? Add your own”.
- The family names the dish, picks when they usually have it (breakfast, lunch, evening snack, dinner) and confirms diet, allergens, onion/garlic and spice, the same check as dishes added later (D03). It is saved as a favourite, kept in the setup draft across refreshes, and becomes one of the family's own dishes (editable in Settings → Dishes you added).

## Behaviour changes to note

- Soy sauce now also counts as **wheat** (it is brewed with wheat), so veg/egg fried rice is blocked for a wheat allergy. More words are recognised for world dishes (pizza, pita, tortilla, miso, tahini, feta…).
- The dish picker used for logging says "Tap everything that was on the plate" instead of the cook's 1st/2nd/backup wording.
