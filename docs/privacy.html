What's Cooking — Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · Applies to the What's Cooking app for Android and iOS (package com.gameon1210.whatscooking)

In short: What's Cooking keeps your family's food information on your phone. We do not run servers that store your data, we have no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics. A few features send limited data to other services only when you use them, as described below.

1. Information you enter

You may enter your family's name, family members' first names and age groups, food rules (for example vegetarian, Jain, fasting days, allergies), food preferences, meals cooked, reactions, leftovers, a shopping list, meal photos, and optionally WhatsApp numbers for yourself and your cook.

This information is stored only on your device in the app's private storage. We (the developer) cannot see it.

3. Permissions

  • Camera and photos — only to photograph or pick a meal when you choose to log one.
  • Notifications — reminders to plan tomorrow, send the Cook Card, and check tiffins. They are scheduled on your phone.
  • Internet — for the weather hint and the optional features above.

4. Children

What's Cooking is meant for adults who plan meals for a household. It is not directed at children. Parents may enter a child's first name, age group and school tiffin preferences; this stays on the parent's device.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with anyone. The only transfers are the user-initiated ones in section 2.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • Export everything as JSON or CSV: Settings → Your data.
  • Delete a family and all its data: Settings → Delete this family. Uninstalling the app also removes all data.
  • Remove your Gemini key any time in Settings.
  • A cook can stop Cook Card messages by tapping “Stop these messages” on the Cook page.

These controls support your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
