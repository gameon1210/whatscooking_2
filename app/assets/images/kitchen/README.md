# Original kitchen photography

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for this app refresh. These are editorial mood photographs used as covers, not representations of a specific suggested dish. No real family photographs were supplied or generated. User-chosen family photos are handled separately and stay in local app storage.

- [shared-table.jpg](shared-table.jpg): 1536 × 1024, JPEG encoded at quality 88 from the original generated PNG.

## Final prompts

**Shared table — photorealistic-natural**

Original wide editorial food photograph for the What’s Cooking family meal-planning app, used as a landscape cover. A beautiful authentic everyday Indian family table that also enjoys global cuisine: a ceramic bowl of warmly coloured vegetable curry, fluffy rice, folded flatbreads, cucumber-tomato salad, a small colourful seasonal vegetable dish, fresh herbs and lemon. Real home-cooked textures, no artificial shine. Sunlit cream linen, pale warm stone, matte cream and muted terracotta ceramics, a sprig of green, relaxed premium cookbook photography. Overhead to slightly angled, generous horizontal composition around 3:2, food fills the scene naturally, enough breathing room for a mobile crop. No lettering, logo, watermark, people or UI. Rich appetising colour, soft natural shadows, tactile real photography.

**Garden counter — photorealistic-natural**

Alternative wide cover photo for a family cooking journal. Fresh ingredients ready for dinner on a pale sage and cream kitchen worktop: an earthy shallow ceramic bowl of cherry tomatoes, lemons cut in half, fresh coriander and basil, a few carrots, a small wooden board, a folded oatmeal linen napkin and subtle olive oil glass. Natural relaxed arrangement, no prepared dish. Premium independent food magazine editorial photography, tactile authentic surfaces, natural morning window light, elegant greens and tomato red with warm creamy neutrals. Landscape 3:2 with breathing room, usable as a wide banner cropped at the middle. No people, text, logos, watermark, UI, packaging or brands.
